It's a stark contrast that besets the current Bitcoin scene - a few months ago, Doomsday vibes were the order of the day, everyday. Is price going to break 50k, or worse still 20k. Pope Saylor was even found shaking, breaking sacred vows and selling pieces of our most holy digital Allspark. Currently, the gloom has passed it seems we may have a break on our hands. 



In the charts below, we have two indicators - a simple moving average - 55 periods and an in-house indicator, the Total Variation Minimization (TVM) indicator. TL:DR, the latter measures chop and tries to determine the behaviour (yes, the -u stays auto-correct) of trends.



![](/assets/BTCUSD_2026-09-30_18-39-06.png)

Courtesy of TradingView



As can be seen above, on the weekly timeframe, the last time price crossed the 55-sma upwards, the next stop was the current all-time-high. Classic break and re-test. Even recently, with the downwards cross, classic break and re-test. The expectation now, with the latest upwards price cross, we may now be building momentum for a shot at another peak. There should not be a surprise if there's a re-test, with the 55-sma acting as support.

The TVM, by trying to measure the scalar distance that price follows - think of the number of steps recorded on your Apple Watch Ultra 4 - against the vector distance that price climbs or falls through - think whether or not you actually make it past base camp - by pitting these price distances against each, the TVM establishes a fractional value for each candle. Against a common threshold, say 0.21 - as with the gold, dashed line above, one may be granted a normalized picture of how much chop or ranging we are fighting with. What we see above is that the TVM values have steadily been decreasing, to the extent that price has been below the golden threshold for a while now. Now, to push things even further, we've drawn a trend-line on those TVM values -- a breakout may be imminent. Please note the TVM line is coloured white throughout -- we determined, for our own ease of reading, that the latest TVM value flushes the whole line with the same colour. So, in this case, with the latest value below the threshold, signaling chop, the line is coloured white.



![](/assets/BTCUSD_2026-09-30_19-28-40.png)

The daily chart, in tandem, argues that the breakout has already started. The flush blue line shows that the golden threshold has been crossed. The descending trend-line on daily TVM values has also been crossed.
