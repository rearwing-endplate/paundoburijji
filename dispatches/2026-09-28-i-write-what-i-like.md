The story goes - that in 1967 - Google can do the fact-checking for us - the South African Mint stumbled upon a first-year university module - Reserve Currencies. They decided it would be a good economic idea to have government issued bullion. And that' s how the Krugerrand was born. Obviously, at the time, Mandela was serving his penance off of the coast of Cape of Supposed Good Hope. With that option unavailable, the task of gracing the obverse side of the new, bullion coin fell to St. Paul himself, still writing to the church of England, sorry, the Church of Netherlands.  
  
At least that was the thought initially, before Siya Kolisi reached out from the future and kicked out the old saint to the reverse side of the coin....
